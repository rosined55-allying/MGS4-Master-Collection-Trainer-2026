## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Snake uses third-person movement with crouching, prone movement, crawling, rolling, leaning, aiming, and context-sensitive actions. Use cover and the environment to stay concealed, and take advantage of OctoCamo, which adapts Snake's camouflage to nearby surfaces. Trainer movement options are best used for exploration or recovery from difficult positions rather than during scripted sequences.

### Tools & Inventory

Weapons and equipment can be collected from battlefields, searched from defeated enemies, or purchased from Drebin's Shop with Drebin Points. Snake can carry multiple firearms, ammunition types, explosives, recovery items, and tactical tools. Activate inventory-related options before purchasing or using supplies, and avoid changing equipment during menus, cutscenes, or scripted weapon transitions.

### World & Environment

The game moves through active war zones where PMC forces, rebel fighters, and other factions can influence the battlefield. Players can sneak through hostile areas, support allied forces, compatibility patrols, or engage enemies directly. The Solid Eye helps identify threats and useful information, while the Threat Ring provides a less intrusive way to read nearby danger. Trainer options can simplify exploration, but major story events and act transitions should be allowed to play normally.

### Combat & Weapons

MGS4 combines stealth, firearms, CQC, tranquilizer weapons, explosives, cover, and close-range takedowns. Snake can aim from multiple perspectives, use suppressors, search fallen enemies, and extension faction conflicts. Monitor health and Psyche during intense encounters, and use the trainer's damage, ammunition, reload, and resource options independently so combat remains as challenging as desired.
